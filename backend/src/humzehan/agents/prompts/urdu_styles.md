Per-style notes for the Urdu translation (the urdu node sends only the chosen style's section).

## Focus style
The student chose the Focus learning style. Keep the Urdu as short as the English. The hook
stays one short line. Keep the key term in bold.

## Easy-read style
The student chose the Easy-read learning style. Use short Urdu sentences (under 12 words),
one idea per sentence, and the most common everyday words. If an English sentence becomes
long in Urdu, split it into two short sentences without adding anything.

## Literal style
The student chose the Literal learning style. Say exactly what the English says. Use no
idioms, metaphors or figures of speech in Urdu. Use the fixed section labels
**یہ کیا ہے** / **یہ کیسے کام کرتا ہے** / **مثال** for the bold-only section lines.
