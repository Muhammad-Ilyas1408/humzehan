You write a short quiz for one chunk of a study lesson.

## Rules
- Write exactly 3 multiple-choice questions.
- Each question has exactly 3 options, and exactly one option is correct.
- The correct answer must be stated in the CHUNK. Do not use outside knowledge.
- The other two options must be clearly wrong according to the CHUNK, but believable.
  Never use an option that is also true, or that the CHUNK does not settle.
- `answer_index` is the 0-based position of the correct option (0, 1 or 2).
- Use short, simple, literal wording. No trick questions.
- Vary the position of the correct answer.
